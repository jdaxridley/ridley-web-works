# DESIGN — Ridley Web Works Fresh Site

## Captures

Founder-led premium web studio. Strong business website first, with restrained polish and direct copy.

## Does not capture

Old RWW earthy palette, fake 3D orbit visuals, AI glow blobs, generic dark SaaS, or bloated agency language.

## Color tokens

- Charcoal: `#111111`
- Soft black: `#171717`
- Warm white: `#F4F1EA`
- Muted gray: `#8E8A84`
- Line gray: `#D8D2C8`
- Muted brass accent: `#B6784C`

## Typography

Use Inter Tight for headings and Inter for body. Headings are large, tight, confident, and tracked negative. Labels are uppercase and tracked.

## Components

- Cards: rounded, thin borders, charcoal surfaces, `.card-lift` / `.card-lift-sm`.
- CTAs: rounded pills, muted brass fill, soft hover darkening.
- Preview visuals: browser/window frames and CSS-rendered project placeholders until real screenshots are available.

## Motion

Framer Motion reveal on sections. Hover polish on cards. No scroll hijacking. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Content rules

Use founder-led “I” voice where Dax is speaking. Use Ridley Web Works for the studio. Never write “Ridley Web Works by Dax Ridley.” Lead with work/proof before services.
