## Convergence — what all three share (the premium DNA to hit)
1. **Restraint in the chrome, boldness in one element.** Nav whispers; one thing shouts (hero image OR headline OR wordmark).
2. **Massive type-size contrast.** The premium feel is 80% "one enormous thing + everything else tiny."
3. **Color as punctuation from the WORK, not painted UI.** Base is neutral (charcoal/cream); color comes from real project imagery.
4. **Tasteful, specific, dated proof.** Not "trusted by many" — actual named work with dates and quiet treatment.
5. **Generous whitespace + slow, heavy motion.** Nothing is busy.
