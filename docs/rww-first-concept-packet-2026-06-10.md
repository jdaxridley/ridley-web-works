## Brief Ingest

Ridley Web Works should present as a capable, founder-led web studio for local and service-based businesses that need stronger websites, clearer direction, and practical digital systems around the site. The site should help a future client trust Dax enough to book a discovery call without pretending RWW is a large agency or inventing proof that does not exist.

Public-facing AI/tooling language should be removed or kept hidden. Placeholder content is allowed only when it is clearly honest scaffolding: project cards, browser frames, future headshot areas, screenshot placeholders, and "case study coming soon" modules are fine; fake testimonials, metrics, awards, results, or client quotes are not.

## Existing Repo Observations

- The repo is already a Next.js 16 / React 19 / Tailwind 4 site with a multi-page app structure.
- Current pages exist for Home, Work, About, and Contact.
- The homepage already leans toward a warm editorial studio direction with Inter Tight, warm white, charcoal, and brass.
- `DESIGN.md` currently prefers a founder-led premium studio direction and explicitly avoids old earthy palettes, fake 3D orbit visuals, AI glow blobs, generic dark SaaS, and inflated agency language.
- Several current details need alignment before final build:
  - CTA language should move from "Start the Build" toward "Book a discovery call."
  - About and Contact still reference Overland Park / Kansas City; the approved brief says Dax is now based in Plano, Texas, with broader North Texas / DFW flexibility.
  - Current home includes a public "AI can make a page..." differentiation section; the approved brief says AI/tooling should stay hidden publicly.
  - Work examples should prioritize Paradise Bakery and Rhythm Cycle & Sculpt where possible. Existing project data includes Rhythm, T-spoon, and Michael Tiplea; these need review against what Dax wants to publicly show.

## Homepage Direction 1: Polished Local Studio

### Visual Feel

Warm, clean, easy to trust. This direction looks like a refined local business partner, not a design-world portfolio and not a SaaS company.

### Palette / Type

- Warm white / soft cream background
- Charcoal typography
- Muted brass accent
- Light gray hairlines
- Inter Tight headings, Inter body

### Homepage Components

- Hero: left-aligned copy, right-side browser-frame collage using honest project/screenshot placeholders.
- Work section: simple cards with industry labels, "needed / built" notes, and no metrics.
- Services: grid of flexible problem areas with concise explanations.
- Founder section: Dax trust block with warm portrait placeholder and direct "work with the person designing your site" copy.
- CTA band: "Book a discovery call" with short pre-call reassurance.

### Sample Hero Copy

> Better websites for local businesses that need to look as good as the work they do.
>
> Ridley Web Works is a focused web studio led by Dax, helping service businesses sharpen their first impression, clarify their offer, and turn their website into something useful again.

Primary CTA: `Book a discovery call`  
Secondary CTA: `View selected work`

### Best For

The clearest path to client trust and near-term launch. It fits home services, wellness, trades, and premium local consumer services without feeling niche-bound.

## Homepage Direction 2: Sharper Creative Web Studio

### Visual Feel

More memorable and design-forward. This direction makes RWW feel sharper, more editorial, and more distinctive while still staying grounded.

### Palette / Type

- Charcoal or near-black hero field
- Warm white content panels
- Brass / clay accent
- Larger typographic scale
- More asymmetrical layouts and layered browser frames

### Homepage Components

- Hero: high-contrast dark intro with oversized type and a strong line like "Your website should carry more of the weight."
- Work section: editorial project rows with larger visual previews.
- Services: fewer, bolder service blocks framed around outcomes: "clarity," "presence," "structure," and "systems."
- Founder section: split panel with Dax copy on a warm surface beside an abstract portrait block.
- CTA area: bold dark footer-style panel.

### Sample Hero Copy

> Websites with enough taste to be remembered and enough structure to sell.
>
> Ridley Web Works helps local and service-based businesses turn scattered pages, dated visuals, and unclear offers into a sharper web presence.

Primary CTA: `Book a discovery call`  
Secondary CTA: `See the process`

### Best For

Making RWW feel more premium and creatively confident. Risk: if pushed too far, it may feel less approachable for practical local business owners.

## Homepage Direction 3: Founder-Led Premium Local Partner

### Visual Feel

Warmer, more personal, and relationship-first. This direction makes Dax the trust anchor sooner and uses the studio brand as the professional wrapper.

### Palette / Type

- Soft cream background
- Charcoal text
- Warm tan / brass accent
- Subtle portrait or silhouette motif
- Rounded cards, calm spacing, less visual noise

### Homepage Components

- Hero: copy emphasizes working directly with Dax, with a tasteful founder/photo placeholder beside a small project preview stack.
- Work section: "Recent builds and studies" with modest cards.
- Services: "What we can work on together" instead of a capabilities grid.
- Founder section: appears higher on the page than in the other concepts.
- CTA area: softer, more conversational invitation.

### Sample Hero Copy

> A better website, built with the person responsible for the work.
>
> Ridley Web Works is a founder-led studio helping local businesses create clearer pages, stronger first impressions, and websites shaped around how they actually sell.

Primary CTA: `Book a discovery call`  
Secondary CTA: `Start with a website review`

### Best For

Building trust quickly and avoiding the fake-agency trap. Risk: if the visual system is too soft, it may feel less commercially ambitious than Dax wants.

## Recommended Direction

Start from **Direction 1: Polished Local Studio**, then borrow selective strength from **Direction 2**.

Why:
- It is the best fit for the approved positioning: founder-led, capable, trustworthy, modern, and not inflated.
- It matches the existing repo's strongest current foundation, so the next prototype can move quickly without throwing away useful work.
- It leaves room to serve North Texas / DFW local businesses while staying broad enough for non-Plano clients.
- It supports honest placeholders naturally through browser frames, project cards, and future case study modules.
- It keeps Dax credible for practical service businesses while still allowing enough polish to feel like RWW has taste.

Recommended adjustment: keep the warm editorial palette and clean cards, but make the homepage slightly bolder through stronger hero typography, sharper project previews, and a more confident final CTA.
